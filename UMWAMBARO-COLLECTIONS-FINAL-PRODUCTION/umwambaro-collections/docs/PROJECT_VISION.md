# Umwambaro Collections

## Vision

Build the most modern, trusted and user-friendly online fashion marketplace in Rwanda.

The platform should make shopping easy for everyone while giving administrators powerful tools to manage products, inventory and orders.

---

## Mission

Offer customers a beautiful shopping experience with fast search, easy checkout and secure payments.

---

## Target Audience

• Men

• Kids

• Fashion Enthusiasts

• Corporate Customers

---

## Business Goals

• Increase online sales

• Promote Rwandan fashion

• Support multiple payment methods

• Mobile-first experience

• Easy inventory management
